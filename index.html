01 / ABOUT

A passion for trains.
A world of simulation.

I’m Tirthesh Bari, based in India, with an interest in railway simulation and the tools, routes, locomotives and experiences that bring virtual railways to life. Through my projects and community activity, I aim to share knowledge, encourage creativity and help more people discover MSTS and Open Rails.

I describe my role as an independent MSTS & Open Rails community ambassador. This is a self-described community role, not a claim of formal appointment or endorsement by the developers or rights holders.
